Important disclosures

What SPP is, and is not.

The Storm Protection Plan is a maintenance and service membership offered by Texas Roof Guardians. It is not an insurance product and does not replace, supplement, or modify your homeowners insurance policy. Membership does not guarantee insurance claim approval, payment, or coverage outcomes. The Loyalty Discount is a contractor pricing incentive applied to qualifying non-insurance repair invoices.
