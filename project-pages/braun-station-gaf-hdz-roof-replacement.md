The tear-off exposed deteriorated decking that was repaired before the home was finished with a GAF Timberline HDZ roofing system.

Multiple areas of deteriorated decking were found along the eaves, including soft spots and open holes that had to be corrected before the new roof could be installed. Close-up photos document that hidden damage.

After repairs, GAF Timberline HDZ shingles created a clean architectural profile. Drone and front-elevation photos show the finished roof from several angles while the tear-off details preserve the workmanship record beneath it.
