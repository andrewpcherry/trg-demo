This Hollywood Park re-roof was documented from tear-off and deck exposure through dry-in and final completion with GAF Timberline HDZ shingles.

The before and process photos show the existing system stripped back to underlayment and decking, followed by exposed sheathing, dry-in and material staging as the new roof was rebuilt.

The finished GAF Timberline HDZ system gives the home a dimensional architectural look, and the photo set clearly records the progression through completed ridges, ventilation details and clean final roof planes.
