SMS Terms

Text messaging program terms and consumer disclosures.

Effective date: September 4, 2026

By voluntarily selecting an SMS consent checkbox and submitting your mobile number, you agree that Texas Roof Guardians may send text messages in the category you selected.

Messages you may receive

Conversational and informational messages may include replies to your inquiry, inspection or appointment confirmations and reminders, estimate or project updates, customer-service messages, and claim-related communications. If you separately consent to marketing messages, you may also receive occasional roofing offers, maintenance reminders, and storm-related promotions.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. After you send STOP, you may receive one confirmation message. Reply HELP for assistance, email info@txroofguardians.com, or call 830-228-6123.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile information, text messaging originator opt-in data, and consent information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information solely on our behalf to operate the messaging program.
