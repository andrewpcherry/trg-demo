Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information you share with us.

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Texas Roof Guardians respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to txroofguardians.com and go.txroofguardians.com and explains the information we may collect when you visit our website, request an inspection or claim review, use our roof assessment, call or text us, or otherwise communicate with our team.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, phone number, email address, property address or ZIP code, details about your roof or property, appointment information, photos or documents you choose to provide, and the content of communications with us.

We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, referral source, and website analytics used to operate and improve our services.

How we use information

We may use your information to respond to inquiries, schedule and manage inspections or appointments, provide estimates or claim-related support, communicate about requested services, send service updates and reminders, improve our website and customer experience, protect against fraud or misuse, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

SMS and mobile messaging privacy

If you voluntarily opt in to receive text messages from Texas Roof Guardians, we may use your mobile number to send the categories of messages described at the point of consent. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out of text messages and HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from Texas Roof Guardians.

Sharing of information

We use service providers to operate our website, communications, scheduling and customer management, including GoHighLevel. They process information on our behalf to provide those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others. The mobile-information and SMS consent restrictions above apply to these disclosures; consent is not transferred for another business's marketing.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting, operational and customer-service requirements.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete information you have provided by contacting us below. We may need to verify your identity and retain records required by law. You may unsubscribe from marketing email using the unsubscribe method provided in those messages. For SMS, reply STOP to opt out. After one opt-out confirmation, we will stop messaging you through that SMS program unless you opt in again. We may respond to your service request by another permitted channel. Submitting a form without SMS consent does not enroll you in text messaging.
