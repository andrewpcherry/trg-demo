Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information you share with us.

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Texas Roof Guardians respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the information we may collect when you visit our website, request an inspection or claim review, use our roof assessment, call or text us, or otherwise communicate with our team.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, phone number, email address, property address or ZIP code, details about your roof or property, appointment information, photos or documents you choose to provide, and the content of communications with us.

We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, referral source, and website analytics used to operate and improve our services.

How we use information

We may use your information to respond to inquiries, schedule and manage inspections or appointments, provide estimates or claim-related support, communicate about requested services, send service updates and reminders, improve our website and customer experience, protect against fraud or misuse, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

SMS and mobile messaging privacy

If you voluntarily opt in to receive text messages from Texas Roof Guardians, we may use your mobile number to send the categories of messages described at the point of consent. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out of text messages and HELP for help.

Mobile information, text messaging originator opt-in data, and consent information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers that process information solely on our behalf to operate our communications and business systems, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

Consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from Texas Roof Guardians.

Sharing of information

We may disclose information to vendors or service providers that help us operate our website, communications, scheduling, customer management, analytics, or other business functions; when required by law; to protect our rights or the safety of others; or in connection with a business transaction. We do not authorize those providers to use your information for their own independent marketing purposes.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting, operational and customer-service requirements.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or correct information you have provided. You may unsubscribe from marketing email using the unsubscribe method provided in those messages. For SMS, reply STOP to opt out. You may still receive non-marketing communications when necessary to respond to a request you initiate or administer an existing service relationship, where permitted by law.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to info@txroofguardians.com or by calling 830-228-6123.
