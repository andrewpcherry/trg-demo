Homeowner education

What every solar homeowner should know before re-roofing.

Solar outlasts most roofs

Solar systems are warrantied 25+ years. Asphalt shingle roofs in Texas average 12 to 18 years with hail. Plan the detach and reset before damage forces it.

Hail damages panels too

Severe Texas hail can crack panel glass, damage microinverters, and dislodge racking, while also destroying the shingles beneath. Always document both systems together.

Coordination protects warranties

An uncoordinated re-roof can void both your roof and your solar warranty. A single accountable contractor eliminates that risk.
